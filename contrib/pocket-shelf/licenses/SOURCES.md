# Native dependency notice sources

- FreeType 2.13.3, bzip2 1.0.8, libpng 1.6.48 and zlib 1.3.1 notices are copied from the corresponding official devkitPro portlib packages in the verified build image.
- HarfBuzz 10.0.1: https://github.com/harfbuzz/harfbuzz/tree/10.0.1
- libnx 4.12.0: https://github.com/switchbrew/libnx/tree/v4.12.0
- Mesa Switch 20.1.0: https://mesa.freedesktop.org/archive
- libdrm_nouveau 1.0.1: https://github.com/devkitPro
- mGBA and its bundled third-party source notices remain available with this fork's source: https://github.com/realzhengyiming/mgba-pocket-shelf

The FreeType distribution includes both FTL and GPL notices; this build uses its permissive FreeType License option. The shelf's font asset has its own separate SIL Open Font License.
