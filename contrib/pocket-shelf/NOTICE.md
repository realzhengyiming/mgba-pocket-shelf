## Boot presentation

The boot sequence is an independent visual recreation, not Nintendo BIOS code or a redistributed video. Timing, letter entrances and the second shine were observed in [arscanum's original hardware startup recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6_ZD3FxMcvQ) ([animated reference](https://makeagif.com/gif/original-game-boy-advance-startup-Mk7uJb)). The recreation does not claim frame-perfect or audio-identical fidelity.

`boot-chime.wav` is this project's synthesized rising flourish/bell approximation (MPL-2.0), generated by `tools/make-boot-chime.py`; it is not a recording or an audio sample extracted from Nintendo's BIOS.
